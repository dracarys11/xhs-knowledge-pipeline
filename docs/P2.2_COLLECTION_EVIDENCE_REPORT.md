## 6. Contract Impact

1. **`docs/P2.2_COLLECTION_CONTRACT.md` Alignment**:
   * Both endpoint paths were observed for this authenticated account on this date; their future availability and untested pagination behavior are not guaranteed.
   * Identifiers (`id`, `note_id`, `name`, `total`) match the contract specification.
   * `data.has_more == false` was observed in one board member response. The report does not establish terminal evidence for every board.
2. **Key Additions for Importer Design**:
   * **Mandatory Auth Guard**: Prior to reading boards, verify `window.__INITIAL_STATE__.user.loggedIn === true`. If false, raise `AuthRequiredError` immediately (never treat `专辑・0` as empty).
   * **Architecture Decision**: Do not rely on direct `fetch()` to `edith.xiaohongshu.com` (which gets HTTP 406 due to missing JS signature headers). Use the proven **Playwright DOM-driven Interaction + Response Interception** model established in P1.
